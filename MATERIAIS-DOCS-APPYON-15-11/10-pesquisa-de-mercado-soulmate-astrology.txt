**Target Audience & Life Stage**
Who they are: Single women, predominantly ages 25–45, university educated, spiritual-leaning, employed. Often post-breakup, post-divorce, or in anxious attachment to emotionally unavailable partners. The psychic services industry in the US is a $2.3 billion market (2026), and over 20% of Americans consult a psychic — with that number growing "when stressed consumers are desperate for answers." Two-thirds of female respondents believe in psychic ability "somewhat or very much." Love and relationships are the #1 topic discussed with psychics.

**KEY BEHAVIORAL PATTERN: The Addiction Cycle**
```
Heartbreak/Loneliness
→ First reading for comfort
→ Positive prediction creates hope (dopamine spike)
→ Reality doesn't match prediction
→ Seek another reading for reassurance
```
