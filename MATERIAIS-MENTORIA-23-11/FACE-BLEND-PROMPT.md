## O prompt (em inglês, como na tela)

```
Use the two uploaded reference images to create a completely new, realistic human face by blending the facial features of both individuals.

Combine their facial characteristics naturally, including face shape, eyes, nose, lips, jawline, cheekbones, and other defining features. Maintain realistic facial proportions, natural skin texture, and balanced symmetry.

The result should look like a completely different person, not a copy of either reference or a simple side-by-side mixture. Make the blending seamless and photorealistic, as if this new person naturally existed.

Preserve realistic human anatomy and avoid artificial-looking features, distortions, or obvious AI artifacts.

Output: One high-quality, photorealistic portrait of the newly created person.
```

## Como usar no nosso fluxo (perfis novos)
1. Escolher 2 fotos de referência (Pinterest ou Pexels) com a idade, o tipo de rosto e o cabelo que a avatar nova deve ter (o Iorhan prefere 20 a 35 anos, aula 67).
2. No Flow (imagem, x4, 0 crédito), anexar as 2 fotos e colar o prompt acima.
3. Escolher a que não lembra nenhuma das 2 fotos; dela sai a folha de personagem (o Blueprint) e o resto do protocolo de avatar nova (`PLANO-MVM-LEGACY-APPYON.md`, "### Configurar uma avatar nova").
