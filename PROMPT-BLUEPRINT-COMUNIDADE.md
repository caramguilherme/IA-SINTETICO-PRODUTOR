Fonte: Nat M., chat-appyon do MVM Legacy, 25/10/2026 19h39, em resposta a quem pediu "o prompt para a criação do blueprint". Copiado como foi postado (o item 26 repetia o 25 e ficou de fora). Uso: anexar UMA foto de referência da avatar e colar este texto no gerador de imagem. Guardado para quando formos fazer uma Nora mais natural, com menos acessórios no rosto (`CLAUDE.md`, seção 6, item 4). Não é instrução para mim: é material de referência.

```
Create a professional, ultra-photorealistic CHARACTER CONSISTENCY SHEET using the uploaded reference image as the ONLY source for the character's identity.
IMPORTANT — IDENTITY LOCK: The uploaded reference image is the definitive identity reference. Reproduce the EXACT SAME PERSON throughout the entire character sheet. Do NOT redesign, beautify, reinterpret, or replace the character. Preserve with maximum accuracy:
* exact facial identity
* facial structure and proportions
* eyes, eye shape and iris color
* eyebrows and natural brow shape
* nose shape and proportions
* lips, mouth shape and fullness
* cheeks, jawline and chin
* skin tone and natural skin texture
* eye appearance
* hairline
* exact hair color, length, texture and hairstyle
* body proportions and physique
* distinctive facial characteristics
CHARACTER SHEET LAYOUT: Create one clean, professional reference-board image containing:
1. LARGE FRONT PORTRAIT / FACE CLOSE-UP
2. FRONT VIEW
3. 3/4 LEFT VIEW
4. 3/4 RIGHT VIEW
5. LEFT PROFILE
6. RIGHT PROFILE
7. BACK VIEW
8. FULL-BODY FRONT VIEW
9. FULL-BODY 3/4 VIEW
10. FULL-BODY SIDE VIEW
11. FULL-BODY BACK VIEW
12. NEUTRAL EXPRESSION
13. NATURAL SMILE
14. SOFT SMILE
15. LOOKING LEFT
16. LOOKING RIGHT
17. EYE CLOSE-UP
18. EYEBROW CLOSE-UP
19. NOSE CLOSE-UP
20. LIPS CLOSE-UP
21. HAIR CLOSE-UP
22. ACCESSORIES CLOSE-UP
23. OUTFIT / CLOTHING REFERENCE
24. COLOR PALETTE / SKIN-TONE REFERENCE
CONSISTENCY: Every panel must show the SAME person. Keep facial identity, proportions, hairstyle, skin tone and physical characteristics consistent across every angle, pose and expression.
For views not clearly visible in the reference, intelligently reconstruct the character while staying strictly faithful to the visible reference. Do not invent major distinguishing features.
PRESENTATION: Premium professional film-production character bible / modeling reference sheet. Clean editorial layout. Neutral studio background. Soft realistic studio lighting. Sharp facial details. Natural skin pores and texture. Individual hair strands. Realistic fabric and accessories. Accurate human anatomy. Consistent camera perspective. Clean thin borders between panels. Professional labels underneath each view. Minimal elegant typography. High-end fashion-model reference aesthetic.
PHOTOREALISM: Ultra-photorealistic. Real human appearance. Natural skin texture. Realistic eyes. Physically accurate hair. Natural shadows. Realistic proportions. No CGI appearance. No plastic skin. No doll-like face. No artificial beauty-face replacement.
NEGATIVE PROMPT: different person, different face, face morphing, identity drift, altered facial structure, different eyes, different nose, different lips, different jawline, different skin tone, different hairstyle, inconsistent body proportions, duplicate person, distorted anatomy, asymmetrical face, plastic skin, waxy skin, CGI, 3D render, cartoon, anime, illustration, over-beautification, excessive makeup, unrealistic eyes, blurry face, low detail, extra fingers, missing fingers, malformed hands, bad anatomy.
OUTPUT: One complete, highly detailed professional CHARACTER CONSISTENCY SHEET in a single image, suitable as a master reference for generating consistent images and videos of this character.
```
