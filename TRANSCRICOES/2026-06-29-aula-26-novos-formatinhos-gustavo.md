Summary
This Loom explains how to create scalable short-form video creatives using Cloud for prompting, NanoBanana Pro, and a GIF generator. The creator walks through generating realistic image prompts for couples and random faces, animating them in VL3, and assembling GIFs for a reaction format with timing in milliseconds and fixed positioning values like X and Y coordinates. They also demonstrate using CapCut effects such as zoom and tracking shot, plus an approach to convert an existing viral Reel into a different audience via Instagram Remix marked as a test. Along the way, they mention Cloud’s $20 cheapest plan, 200,000 tokens, and up to 300 messages per day, and note that remixing other people’s videos can risk being removed from the project and having balances frozen.
